## [Arithmetic Circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic_circuit_complexity#Definitions)

An **arithmetic circuit** $C$ over the [_field_](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_(mathematics)>) $F$ and the set of variables $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ is a [_directed acyclic graph (DAG)_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directed_acyclic_graph) with

- Every _node_ (a.k.a. _gate_) with _indegree_ zero is called an _input gate_ and is labeled by either a variable $x_i$ or a field element in $F$.
- Every non-input gate is labeled by either $+$ (_sum gate_) or $\times$ (_product gate_).
